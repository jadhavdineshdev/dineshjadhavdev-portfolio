HealthChain

Feb 2026 to now · Full-stack developer

A healthcare records platform with separate portals for patients, doctors and administrators. Patients decide who can open their records. Doctors have to pass a license check first, and anyone can sign in with Google.

Access is role-based. Medical data stays off-chain, and audit proofs are written to Hyperledger Fabric and checked with SHA-256, so tampering shows up.

Built with React, Node.js, Express, Hyperledger Fabric, Docker and Tailwind CSS.
